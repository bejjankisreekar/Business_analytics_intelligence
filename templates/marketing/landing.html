Your data, your choice

Choose where your data lives

At signup, you pick one of two places for your business data to live — nothing shared, nothing mixed with any other organization on the platform either way.

Your own Google Drive

Your numbers live in a real Google Sheet, created in your own Google Drive — not ours. Open it, scroll through it, export it, read it with your own eyes at any time, from any device you're signed into.

  • ✓ You own the file — it's in your Drive, under your Google account
  • ✓ We never store a copy of your financial data ourselves
  • ✓ We create one Sheet inside your Drive and only ever touch that file — we never get access to the rest of your Drive or any other financial data of yours

Prism Pulse's own database

Prefer not to connect a Google account at all? Your data lives in our own managed database instead — isolated to your organization alone, ready the instant you sign up.

  • ✓ No Google account needed — works immediately after signup
  • ✓ Your data is never visible to, or shared with, any other organization
  • ✓ Export to Excel any time, straight from your profile

You pick one at signup, and you can switch between them any time afterward from your profile — your existing data moves with you either way.
