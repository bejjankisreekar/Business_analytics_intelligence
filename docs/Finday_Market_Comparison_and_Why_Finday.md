## The problem

Most owner-run businesses know their totals only at month end. Sales sit in a notebook, purchases in a bill folder, expenses in a WhatsApp group, and the accountant gets everything late. By the time the numbers arrive, the decisions that needed them have already been made.

Finday closes that gap. Log each day's sales, purchases and expenses in seconds, and see where the business stands the same day.

## The options in the market

Business owners in India usually choose between a few kinds of tools. Each solves part of the problem.

| Option | Typical cost | What it does well | Where it falls short for daily profit visibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spreadsheets (Excel, Google Sheets) | Free | Flexible, familiar, no subscription | Built and maintained by hand; no guided entry, no automatic reports, errors grow with the data |
| Khatabook | Not confirmed (no reliable current price found) | Simple customer credit (udhaar) tracking on mobile | Focused on credit balances, not full profit, balance sheet or cash flow |
| myBillBook and Vyapar | Free or limited free plans; paid plans from about INR 399 to 4,000 a year (vendor and third-party figures) | GST billing, invoices, inventory, mobile use, some work offline | Built around billing and invoices; profit, ledger and cash analytics are secondary |
| Zoho Books and Wave | Free tiers with limits; paid plans from about USD 16 to 20 a month | Complete accounting, invoicing, bank feeds | Accounting-first, so entering daily sales is heavier; free tiers limit users, invoices or bank feeds |
| Tally Prime | Silver single-user about INR 22,500 plus GST, plus about INR 4,500 a year for updates (third-party figures) | Industry standard for Indian accountants | Desktop software that needs accounting knowledge; not designed for owner dashboards |
| GnuCash | Free and open source | Full double-entry accounting, works offline | Desktop only, no hosted staff logins, manual bank imports, steep learning curve |

Prices change often and come from public pricing pages and third-party reviews checked in October 2026. Check each vendor's site before a purchase decision.

## Why Finday

**1. Built for the owner's daily decisions.**
Entries take seconds, and the dashboard updates from them the same day. You see margins, revenue drivers and cost trends without waiting for month end.

**2. Your data stays in your hands.**
By default, your finance data lives in a Google Sheet inside your own Google Drive. Finday reads and writes it live and does not keep a copy in its database. You can open, download or delete the Sheet yourself, and remove Finday's access from your Google account at any time. Finday asks for the limited `drive.file` permission only, so it cannot browse the rest of your Drive.

**3. One set of numbers for you and your accountant.**
P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow and GST summary come from the same entries. Export them as PDF, Excel or CSV. Your accountant works from the same figures you see, with no re-keying.

**4. Your team, with the right access.**
Owners, admins, managers and members each have their own login. Managers can be limited to the pages they need, so staff see only what their role allows.

**5. Made for Indian businesses.**
INR currency, GST reporting, financial-year start month you choose, and Indian digit grouping in every amount.

**6. Referral commissions built in.**
Track commissions due and paid per referrer, which general accounting and billing tools do not cover.

**7. Flexible storage, with control.**
Choose your own Google Drive (default) or Finday's managed database. Switching between them is a request that the Finday team reviews, with the billing impact shown first.

## Where a simpler tool may fit better

Finday is a good fit when you want daily visibility into profit and cash. Other tools may suit you better if:

- You only need to send invoices and collect GST on them. A billing app is cheaper.
- You need full double-entry accounts for statutory audit. Tally or a full accounting package is the right choice.
- You work without internet for long periods. Offline billing apps or desktop software are better suited.
- You only track customer credit. A credit ledger app is enough.

We would rather you pick the right tool than the wrong one with us.
